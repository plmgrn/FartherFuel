FartherFuel

Compares filling up at a nearby station with driving farther for a lower price.

Inputs

Chosen point (also set by the map)
Vehicle
Stations
Map

Model

savings = V*(Pn - Pf) + C/100*(Dn*Pn - Df*Pf) + k*(Dn - Df)

V  fuel bought (L)            C  consumption (L/100 km)
Pf farther price (EUR/L)      Pn nearer price (EUR/L)
Df farther extra km (total)   Dn nearer extra km (total)
k  other cost per extra km (EUR/km)

Fuel burned on a detour is assumed to be replaced at that station’s price.
